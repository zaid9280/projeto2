Stranger Things is an American science-fiction horror television series created by the Duffer Brothers for Netflix. The Setting • Hawkins, Indiana (1980s): The primary setting is the fictional, quiet Midwestern town of Hawkins, Indiana, during the 1980s. The atmosphere is deeply inspired by 1980s pop culture, horror and adventure tropes, and the works of Steven Spielberg and Stephen King. • Expanded Locations: While Hawkins remains the central hub, later seasons expand the setting to other locations such as California and Russia, as well as real-world-inspired underground or classified government facilities like Hawkins National Laboratory. • The Upside Down: An eerie, toxic, and dark alternate dimension that mirrors Hawkins in a state of decay, overgrown with vine-like organisms, ash-like spores, and hive-mind entities.
