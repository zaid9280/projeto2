Stranger Things is a global pop-culture phenomenon created by the Duffer Brothers for Netflix. Over its historic run, the main series spanned 5 seasons and 42 episodes, officially concluding with an epic, feature-length series finale on New Year's Eve, December 31, 2025. While the flagship story of Eleven and the Hawkins crew has come to an end, the Stranger Things Universe is actively expanding into an interconnected franchise of live-action spin-offs, animated series, and stage plays. 🚀 The Next Era: Brand New Live-Action Spin-off Announced officially on Stranger Things Day (November 6, 2026), Netflix has ordered a massive new live-action expansion: • Title: Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure • Release Window: Targeted for 2028 • The Setting: Set in April 1955 in the town of Marian, Nevada. • The Plot & Mythology: It moves away from the 1980s Hawkins timeline to explore an entirely new ensemble of heroes and villains. The Duffers have teased that it will build its own distinct mythology while finally answering massive lore questions lingering from the main series—including the origins of a certain cave scientist and the mysterious glowing rock found by Henry Creel. • The Creative Team: Kate Trefry (veteran writer of the flagship series) will step up as showrunner. Acclaimed filmmaker Joe Wright is signed on to direct the first three episodes, and Shawn Levy returns to executive produce.
